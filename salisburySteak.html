Salisbury Steak

Ingredients Measurement
bread crumbs 1/2 cup
egg 1
ketchup 2 tbsp
Worcestershire sauce 2 tbsp
butter 1 tbsp
mustard 1 tsp
ground beef 1 lb
mushrooms 1 cup
onion 1
beef broth 2 cups

Directions

Make the Patties

In a large bowl, combine bread crumbs, egg, 1 tbsp ketchup, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, and mustard. Gently mix in ground beef. Divide into 4 patties.

Sear the Steaks

Melt butter in skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the patties for 2-3 minutes per side until a nice crust forms. Remove and set them aside on a plate (they will finish cooking in the gravy).

Build the Mushroom and Onion Gravy

In the same skillet, add a little extra butter, then saute mushrooms and onion until soft and golden brown. Whisk in beef broth, 1 tbsp ketchup, and 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce. Bring it to a simmer until the gravy thickens.

Combine and Finish

Slip the patties back into the skillet and coat them in the gravy. Cover the pan and let it simmer for 5-10 minutes.
