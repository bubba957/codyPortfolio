Potato Gratin

Ingredients Measurement
potatoes 3lbs
milk 2 cups
garlic 2 cloves
butter 2 tbsp
cheese 1.5 cups
thyme 3 leaves

Directions

Preheat your oven to 350°F. Warm the milk, mince the garlic, and melt the butter into a bowl. Grease a baking dish. Layer half of the thinly sliced potatoes, pour some of the cream mixture over them, and sprinkle with a layer of cheese and thyme. Repeat while saving a generous handful of cheese for the end. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 90 minutes. Remove the foil, scatter the remaining cheese, and bake for another 10 minutes.
