Garlic Shrimp Spaghetti

Ingredients Measurement
spaghetti 8 oz
shrimp 1 lbs
butter 4 tbsp
garlic 4 cloves
lemon juice 2 tbsp
parsley 2 tbsp

Directions

Cook the spaghetti in a large pot of boiling water. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining. Melt 2 tbsp of butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side until pink and opaque. Remove the shrimp and set them aside. In the same skillet, lower the heat to medium and add the remaining 2 tbsp of butter and the garlic. Cook for 1 minute. Stir in the reserved pasta water to build the sauce. Toss the spaghetti and shrimp back into the skillet. Garnish with parsley and serve.
