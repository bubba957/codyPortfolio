Key Lime Cheesecake

Ingredients Measurement
graham cracker crumbs 1 1/2 cups
butter 6 tbsp
cream cheese 24 ounces
white sugar 1 cup
lime zest 1 tbsp
cornstarch 1 tbsp
eggs 3
key lime juice 2/3 cups

Directions

Make the Crust

Mix the graham cracker crumbs and melted butter in a bowl. Press the mixture into the bottom of a springform pan. Place the pan in the refrigerator to chill.

Prepare the Filling

Beat the cream cheese, lime zest, and cornstarch in a bowl with an electric mixer. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing until combined. Turn the mixer to low while slowly pouring in the key lime juice.

Bake the Cheesecake

Pour the filling into your graham cracker crust. Place a shallow pan filled halfway with hot water on the lower oven rack to create steam and prevent the top from cracking. Preheat your oven to 300F. Bake for 55-65 minutes. Turn off the oven, open the door slightly, and let the cheesecake sit for 30 minutes.

Chill and Serve

Remove cheesecake from oven. Refrigerat 8 hours (or overnight) before slicing. Garnish with wipped cream or extra lime zest if desired.
